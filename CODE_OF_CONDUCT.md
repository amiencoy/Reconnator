# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors, maintainers, and community members, pledge to make participation
in the Reconnator project a welcoming, safe, and respectful experience for everyone.

We are committed to an environment that respects the dignity, rights, perspectives,
and contributions of all people, regardless of age, body size, disability,
neurodiversity, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or other status.

Everyone who participates in good faith and follows this Code of Conduct is entitled
to the same opportunity to contribute and participate.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy project community include:

- Being respectful, patient, and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews,
  discussions, and other project spaces.
- Respecting differing viewpoints, technical approaches, backgrounds, and levels
  of experience.
- Giving and accepting technical feedback without making it personal.
- Using clear and inclusive language, especially when communicating across
  different cultures or native languages.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing when appropriate, and working
  to repair any harm caused.
- Crediting the work, ideas, research, and sources contributed by others.
- Prioritizing the safety and well-being of the community and the responsible use
  of security tooling.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable in Reconnator community spaces:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Insults, personal attacks, threats, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory
  comments.
- Discrimination, stereotyping, or exclusion based on identity, background, or
  personal characteristics.
- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances.
- Publishing or threatening to publish another person's private information
  without explicit permission.
- Impersonating another person or using misleading identities to evade
  moderation.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews, releases, or other project work.
- Submitting malicious code, hidden destructive behavior, credential-stealing
  logic, or contributions intended to facilitate unauthorized activity.
- Pressuring contributors to disclose sensitive target, client, vulnerability,
  or personal information.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate or harmful in
  a professional open-source community.

## Security Research and Responsible Use

Reconnator is intended for authorized reconnaissance and security assessment.
Technical discussions and contributions must not be used to coordinate
unauthorized access, exploitation, destructive testing, harassment, or harm.

Security findings that could expose users or systems should not be posted in a
public issue. Follow the project's private vulnerability disclosure process once
it is documented in `SECURITY.md`.

This section does not replace the project's security policy or usage terms. It
defines the behavioral expectations for participating in the project community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all official Reconnator community spaces,
including the repository, issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions,
documentation, and project-managed communication channels.

It also applies when someone officially represents Reconnator in public, such as
through an official account, email address, event, presentation, or other
appointed role.

Do not report sensitive conduct incidents through a public GitHub issue.

When possible, include:

- A description of what happened.
- Links, screenshots, logs, or other relevant context.
- The date and location of the incident.
- Whether the behavior is ongoing.
- Any immediate safety or confidentiality concerns.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably
possible. Information will only be shared when necessary to investigate the
report, protect affected people, or carry out an enforcement decision. Retaliation
against anyone who makes a good-faith report or participates in an investigation
is prohibited.

If the project grows to include additional maintainers, a maintainer who is
personally involved in a report must not make the enforcement decision alone.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. Responses will be based on the context, severity, impact, and pattern of
the behavior. Depending on the circumstances, steps may be skipped.

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** Inappropriate language or behavior with limited impact.

**Consequence:** A private request to correct the behavior, edit or remove
content, and clarify future expectations.

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** A clear violation or repeated inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A formal private warning. Conditions may be placed on continued
participation, including restrictions on particular interactions.

### 3. Temporary Restriction

**Impact:** A serious violation or repeated behavior after a warning.

**Consequence:** Temporary restriction from project interaction, which may
include issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, or other community spaces.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Impact:** Severe harassment, threats, deliberate harm, malicious activity, or a
pattern of violations that previous enforcement has not resolved.

**Consequence:** Permanent removal from project community spaces where technically
and administratively possible.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull
requests, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Enforcement
decisions should be documented privately and applied consistently.
